# Revision 02: editorial direction and scroll sequence

This revision gives The Pass a more distinctive hospitality identity and connects the page into a sequence of readable chapters. It retains the consulting positioning, forest/ivory/brass palette, and inquiry path. A full copy-editing round remains pending the owner's next instructions.

## Review findings and changes

| Finding | Implemented change |
| --- | --- |
| Repeated heading, paragraph, and equal-card layouts made the page feel assembled from a template. | Varied section proportions, open service rows, a vertical process, and a text-led founder section establish a clearer editorial rhythm. |
| Similar spacing and boxed sections interrupted the reading sequence. | An overlapping ivory chapter edge, continuous rules, and contrasting forest chapters connect the sections. Desktop sticky elements support the sequence. |
| The abstract architectural sculpture had little connection to restaurant operations. | An original procedural place setting uses a ceramic plate, brass cutlery, and glass tumbler. The scene responds gently to the pointer. |
| Small descriptive text and repeated interface decoration competed with the message. | Larger body text, a clearer type hierarchy, and fewer ornamental elements improve reading. Glass effects remain concentrated in the navigation, tumbler, and inquiry panel. |
| Repeated calls to action added noise. | The hero has one primary action; service rows share a closing inquiry link. |
| Fictional charts could suggest a product demo or business evidence. | The selectable deliverable preview now shows document contents. Its illustrative-layout caption remains explicit. No results, testimonials, or case studies were invented. |

## Page sequence

1. **Opening:** “Good hospitality. Stronger business.” introduces the practice beside the place setting, with an inquiry action and a link into the page.
2. **Expertise:** four open rows cover operational diagnostics, staffing, costs, and sales/guest demand.
3. **Approach:** Listen, Understand, Prioritize, and Put into practice form a vertical reading sequence. A desktop heading and decorative progress marker provide context.
4. **Deliverables:** visitors can select the diagnostic, roadmap, and conversation previews to understand the proposed handoff.
5. **The person behind The Pass:** Michael Park's role, approach, and geographic openness provide context for the engagement.
6. **Questions and inquiry:** native expandable answers address fit, scope, cost, and initial information; the existing form and direct email complete the journey.

## Motion contract

Scrolling remains native. There is no scroll takeover, mandatory animation sequence, or continuous decorative loop. Headline lines, selected blocks, and rules enter once as they become visible. The desktop hero artwork has shallow scroll movement; the sculpture has restrained pointer response without automatic spinning.

Content is server-rendered and visible before enhancement. Reduced-motion preferences disable entrances, parallax, animated transitions, and sticky chapter staging. Compact or short viewports use ordinary section flow and omit hero parallax; the mobile process remains a readable vertical list. The sculpture provides a static fallback when WebGL is unavailable. Motion handlers and observers are cleaned up on unmount.
