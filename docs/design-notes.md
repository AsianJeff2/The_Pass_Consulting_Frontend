# Design and content decisions

The public site presents The Pass as a hospitality consulting practice for independent restaurant operators. Southern California is a home base, not a service boundary. Fees and scope are discussed after an inquiry.

## Visual direction

Warm ivory `#f5f3eb`, forest `#193e32`, deep forest `#102d24`, and muted brass `#78613c`. Light brass `#d6c49a` supports large lettering on forest backgrounds. Editorial serif headings sit alongside sans-serif navigation and 16px body text. System font stacks avoid third-party font requests. Thin rules and varied spacing establish the reading rhythm.

Glass is concentrated in the navigation, the 3D tumbler, and the inquiry panel. The original place setting responds gently to pointer position. It does not spin continuously, intercept touch scrolling, or carry essential information. Reduced-motion users receive a stationary composition, and unavailable WebGL receives an original SVG alternative.

Revision 02 replaces the abstract arches with a turned ceramic plate, brass cutlery, and a glass tumbler. Open service rows replace cards. The approach unfolds vertically alongside a sticky desktop heading and a decorative progress indicator. Overlapping chapter edges, a shallow hero parallax, and once-only headline entrances connect sections. Small screens, short windows, and reduced-motion settings use ordinary document flow. All text is visible in the server-rendered HTML before enhancement.

## References inspected

The following Minimal Gallery previews informed the composition. No template was purchased, imported, or copied.

- [Aurevia](https://minimal.gallery/template/aurevia/): editorial type paired with a single sculptural 3D focal point.
- [Oakline](https://minimal.gallery/template/oakline/): compact navigation, restrained overlays, and a premium presentation.
- [SAVORY](https://minimal.gallery/template/savory/): warm backgrounds, legible serif hierarchy, and a hospitality-adjacent tone.

For the editorial revision, the live [NKORA website](https://nkora.co.uk), found through [Minimal Gallery](https://minimal.gallery/nkora/), informed large typography and full-width hospitality sections. [Onœra](https://onoera.com), also [listed in the gallery](https://minimal.gallery/onoera/), was inspected for section continuity and staged entrances. Its long opening animation was not adopted. References informed principles; their copy, assets, and code were not imported.

UI/UX Pro Max was used for the initial review, spacing, responsive hierarchy, motion/accessibility constraints, and Next.js client boundaries. Its automatic suggestion of testimonials and a blue palette was rejected because it did not fit the evidence or the approved identity. The website has no testimonial content.

## Content boundaries

The website draws from the practice's active brief, marketing positioning, diagnostic instrument, deliverable definitions, and communication workflow specifications. It presents operational diagnosis, labor/staffing patterns, operating costs, and guest/sales demand as areas for discussion.

The internal production engine is separate. Its benchmark fixtures are synthetic and are not presented as client work. No testimonials, client logos, quantified results, outcome guarantees, internal costs, or promised delivery deadlines have been invented. The document explorer is visibly labeled as an illustrative layout.

The first inquiry collects enough context for Michael to respond. Operational records and confidential uploads belong in a later conversation through an appropriate channel. No founder credentials beyond the documented role have been added.
